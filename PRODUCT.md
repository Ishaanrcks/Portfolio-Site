# Product

## What this is
A one-page personal portfolio for Ishan Yash Patel, an Economics undergraduate at IIT Kharagpur who does applied AI / LLM-agent research and engineering.

## Audience
Research supervisors, hiring managers and recruiters skimming on a laptop or phone. They need to see within seconds: who he is, where he has researched/worked, what he has built, and how to reach him.

## Mode
Experience (portfolio): the work leads; the interface recedes.

## Tone and design direction
Minimal, dark, Vercel-like. Type-led, monochrome with one restrained accent. No card grids, kickers, section numbers or gradient text.

## Constraints
- Content comes strictly from the CV; never invent claims. Unknowns are marked `TODO` in `src/data/portfolio.ts`.
- Next.js App Router + Tailwind + TypeScript + Framer Motion, deployable on Vercel.
- Accessible: AA contrast, keyboard focus, reduced-motion respected.
